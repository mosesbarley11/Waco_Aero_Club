Project 02

Introduction to CAD Design

FALL 2025 CAD / DESIGN COMPLETED
Overview

A collaborative intro course led by the President and Vice President of the Baylor University Aerospace Club. Members were walked through the fundamentals of computer-aided design — from reading engineering drawings and setting up a workspace, to modeling basic components from scratch. The session gave the club a foundation for designing parts in-house for future projects.
